Your Shadow™ is the operational version of you that moves with you: handling tasks, decisions, and interfaces — while preserving continuity across years.

  • Private continuity: long-term context stored locally, not harvested.

Core definition

Personal AI Shadow™ = Private • Persistent • Identity-bound • Device-aware • Action-capable.

It exists to eliminate friction, replace app sprawl, preserve your priorities, and become a stable interface to life — without surrendering custody.

Identity Layer

Learns your decision style and non-negotiables so it resolves conflicts the way you would.

  • Communication style alignment

Operational Layer

  • Automation and scripting

Custody Layer
