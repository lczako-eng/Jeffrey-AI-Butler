Digital Conscience

Digital Conscience is a persistent hierarchy of your priorities and decision rules—stored separately from temporary reasoning—so your values persist across years, models, and devices.

Read the Whitepapers See Self-Cloud™ How it works

One-line definition

Values preserved separately from intelligence

Reasoning can change. Your hierarchy shouldn’t.

What it stores

  • Priority hierarchies (what wins when values collide)
  • Decision rules (“when X conflicts with Y, choose Y”)
  • Corrections that become durable guidance

Why it matters

Memory without values is not continuity

Most systems preserve information, but they don’t preserve your value hierarchy as a distinct structure. That’s why assistants feel inconsistent: they might “remember facts” but forget what you consider non-negotiable.

Digital Conscience turns corrections into durable guidance. Over time, JEFFEREY learns what you reject, what you insist on, and what must never happen—even if it’s “convenient.”

privacy > convenience safety > speed truth > comfort when priorities collide: choose the higher rule

What this prevents

  • Drift: behavior changing when the underlying model changes
  • Amnesia of priorities: remembering facts but ignoring boundaries
  • Account custody: values locked inside vendor policy instead of your hardware

Digital Conscience is designed to persist by custody, not by terms of service.

Structure

A persistent layer with a simple interface

The point is not complexity. The point is separation: keep conscience durable and reasoning disposable. That makes the system stable across time.

Core separation

  • Conscience: stored rules + hierarchy + boundaries (durable)
  • Reasoning: temporary inference and planning (replaceable)
  • Audit: logs that show why the system did what it did (inspectable)
