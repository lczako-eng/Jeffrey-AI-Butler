Questions

Frequently asked

What is a Personal AI Shadow™?
A private, long-lived personal AI designed for one person. It preserves continuity over time and stays under your authority via owned infrastructure rather than vendor custody. One human, one shadow.
Who is JEFFEREY for?
One person at a time — and especially the people technology usually leaves behind: the elderly, the isolated, the overwhelmed. Jefferey is companion-first and protector-first: emotionally present, watchful against scams and predatory billing, and loyal for life.
How is JEFFEREY different from Siri, Alexa, or ChatGPT?
Assistants answer questions. JEFFEREY represents you: he learns your priority hierarchy through your corrections, explains recommendations in terms of your own values, and proactively works toward goals you've approved — within permissions you control.
Does JEFFEREY compete with Claude or GPT?
No — he rides them. JEFFEREY is designed as a permanent personal layer that plugs into the best AI engines through open agent standards. The intelligence is rented; the conscience — your memories, priorities, and history — is owned by you and moves between engines.
What is Self-Cloud™?
Owned storage and compute acting as a private custody layer for your Digital Conscience. When powered down, it has no network presence — existence becomes deliberate.
How does Digital Conscience differ from normal AI memory?
It's a persistent hierarchy of your priorities and decision rules — stored separately from temporary reasoning, with confidence scores you can inspect, correct, and delete — so your values remain durable guidance over time.
