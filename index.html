Digital Conscience: values preserved separately from intelligence.

Most AI systems store “knowledge.” They do not preserve your personal value hierarchy as a distinct, persistent structure. Jefferey is built around a different split: temporary reasoning (disposable intelligence) and permanent conscience (your priorities).

Your Digital Conscience is formed through your corrections — what you reject, what you insist on, and what you rank as non-negotiable. Over time, Jefferey learns not just what you like, but what you value most when priorities collide.

What your Digital Conscience stores

  • Priority hierarchies. Ranked principles — safety, privacy, time, financial limits, personal rules — so when values collide, the system knows which one wins because you defined it.
  • Decision patterns. Structured reasoning such as “when X conflicts with Y, choose Y.” Your corrections become durable logic, not temporary preferences.
  • Long-term continuity. Years of context preserved on owned hardware — not tied to accounts, subscriptions, or policy changes.

This is the foundation of a Personal AI Shadow™ you can trust: decisions shaped by your value structure, not generic engagement optimization.

Physical Sovereignty: the strongest security posture is non-existence.
